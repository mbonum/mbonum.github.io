Quotes to keep in mind from books.

Collision by Eric C. So
`We must become mindful architects rather than passive consumers.`

`Every choice about what to outsource is a choice about what to become.`

`If we cannot think without the machine, we are not thinking at all.`

`What we cultivate, we keep. What we outsource, we lose.`

`Each time you use AI to do your thinking rather than to enhance your thinking, you're not just taking a shortcut. You're training yourself to need shortcuts. You're building dependence rather than capability.`

This book made clear something I felt unconsciously, that the writing  fluency I had, especially during the master's thesis work has atrophied because pursuing my proclivity to perfectionism, using the right words or phrasing, I made the mistake to outsource my thought to AI to the point that now I spend more time to answer emails without AI.
After reading this book, I started to write by hand again, questions and ideas that I want to dig deeper into.
